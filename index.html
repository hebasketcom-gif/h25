About Dr. Praveen Pathak

Dr. Praveen Pathak

Head of Department (HOD)

Department of History, Starex University

Dr. Praveen Pathak is an accomplished academic, Head of the History Department, and Assistant Professor at Starex University. As a dedicated scholar and educator, Dr. Pathak brings deep commitment to historical research, academic rigor, and undergraduate and postgraduate history education.

Holding a doctorate (PhD) alongside an MPhil, MA in History, and qualifying prestigious national credentials including UGC-JRF, NET, and SET (History), Dr. Pathak exemplifies academic scholarship and pedagogical leadership. His work focuses on guiding students, encouraging critical historical analysis, and fostering a deep understanding of historical inquiry.
