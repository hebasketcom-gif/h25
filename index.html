Academic Excellence & Leadership

Dr. Praveen Pathak serves as the Head of Department (HOD) and Assistant Professor in the History Department at Starex University. As a distinguished academician and scholar, Dr. Pathak brings an extensive foundation of rigorous research and qualification to university-level instruction and departmental governance.

With advanced research degrees including a Doctorate (PhD) and Master of Philosophy (MPhil), alongside prestigious national recognitions such as the Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), National Eligibility Test (NET), and State Eligibility Test (SET in History), his academic career reflects a steadfast commitment to historical scholarship and knowledge sharing.
