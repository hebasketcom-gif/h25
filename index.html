About Dr. Praveen Pathak

Dr. Praveen Pathak
HOD & Assistant Professor, History Department
Starex University

A distinguished scholar and academic administrator committed to advancing historical education and scholarly research.

Dr. Praveen Pathak serves as the Head of the Department (HOD) and Assistant Professor in the Department of History at Starex University. Holding prestigious qualifications including a PhD, MPhil, JRF (Junior Research Fellowship), NET, SET (History), and MA (History), Dr. Pathak brings exemplary academic credentials and intellectual depth to university education.

Dr. Pathak's academic focus spans multiple fundamental areas of historical inquiry: broad History, Medieval History, Gandhi Studies, and Hindu Studies. Through pedagogical leadership, course development, and research mentorship, Dr. Pathak inspires students to examine historical transformations, primary sources, and ideological shifts with analytical precision.
